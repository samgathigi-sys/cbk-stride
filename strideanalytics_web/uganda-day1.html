EAPS Settlement Finality & The Wire Hijack Vector

2 Contact Hours
📜 1. Settlement Finality (NPS Act § 18)

Under Section 18 of the Uganda National Payment Systems Act 2020, once an RTGS instruction is settled in central bank funds, it is irrevocable. Central bankers have zero margin for error; money cannot be pulled back once stamped final.

📦 2. SWIFT MT103 vs ISO 20022 pacs.008

Legacy SWIFT MT messages used rigid telex text lines. Modern ISO 20022 wires use structured XML trees (pacs.008). This offers richer audit trails, but also exposes XML payload tags to unauthorized in-transit mutation.

⚖️ 3. Computer Misuse Act § 19

Uganda Computer Misuse Act 2011/2022 § 19 criminalizes unauthorized interception and alteration of electronic financial data. Establishes legal authority to demand forensic logs from commercial bank participants.

KEY TAKEAWAY FOR TODAY:

Never trust what the transaction summary says on your screen. Always verify the cryptographic math (SHA-256 payload checksum) before clearing multi-billion shilling wire batches.
