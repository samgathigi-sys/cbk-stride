## 1. Uganda Legal Framework for Cyber Forensics
- **Computer Misuse Act, 2011 & Amendment 2022**:
  - Section 12: Unauthorized access to computer programs or data.
  - Section 14: Access with intent to commit or facilitate commission of further offence.
  - Section 15: Unauthorized modification of computer contents.
  - Section 20: Electronic fraud.
  - Section 24: Unsolicited cyber transmissions and identity theft.
- **Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019**:
  - Lawful processing of personal financial identifiers.
  - Chain of custody data handling ensuring no unauthorized privacy leaks during investigation.
- **Electronic Signatures Act, 2011**:
  - Authenticity of cryptographic hashes as legal proof of non-tampering.

## 2. Evidence Admissibility in Ugandan Courts
To satisfy judicial scrutiny under the Uganda Evidence Act:
1. **Authenticity**: Proof that the evidence is what the investigator purports it to be.
2. **Integrity**: Demonstrable chain of custody with uncompromised cryptographic hashes.
3. **Reliability**: Use of scientifically validated, peer-reviewed forensic methodologies.
4. **Competence of Investigator**: Demonstrated qualifications, documented procedures, and absence of investigator bias.
