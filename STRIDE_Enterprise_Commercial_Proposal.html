The Enterprise Challenge: Why Traditional AGMs & Events Fail

Every year, corporate secretaries, SACCO boards, and event committees waste millions of shillings renting on-premise hardware, battling chaotic paper registration desks, and managing disputed voting tallies—all while exposing attendee personal phone numbers in direct violation of the Kenya Data Protection Act 2019.

⚠️ The Quorum Integrity Vulnerability

Manual physical ledger headcounts lead to bitter quorum disputes. Without real-time verification of principal delegates vs. unaccredited proxies, AGM resolutions risk being invalidated under Companies Act § 284 and SASRA regulations.

⚖️ ODPC § 25 Privacy Breach Liability

Open paper sign-in registers at venue usher desks display executive and member phone numbers to the entire auditorium, triggering statutory fines of up to KES 5,000,000 or 1% annual turnover by the Data Commissioner.

🗳️ Secret Ballot & Proxy Tampering

Paper ballot slips allow rogue proxies to vote without share-weighted authorization. Manual ballot box tallies lack cryptographic audit trails, inviting contentious election petitions.

💸 Exorbitant Infrastructure Capex

Renting proprietary badge printers, local servers, badge lanyards, and specialized scanning wands routinely drains between KES 500,000 and KES 1,500,000 for a single one-day event.
