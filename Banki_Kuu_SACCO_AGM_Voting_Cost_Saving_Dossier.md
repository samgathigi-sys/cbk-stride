## 2. 🔄 Step-by-Step Simulation Guide

### Step 1: Member Self-Registration & Accreditation
1. Access the online portal at **`https://cbk-stride.streamlit.app/EVENTS`**.
2. Select **Statutory AGM Shareholder Accreditation Mode**.
3. Select your member status (e.g., *Ordinary Member / Shareholder* or *Independent Auditor / SASRA Observer*).
4. Enter your **Member Account Number** (e.g., `SACCO-3428`) and **National ID / Email**.
5. Select your **Voting Power / Share Bracket** (1-Person-1-Vote or Proportional Share Capital).

---

### Step 2: Instant M-Pesa Verification & Mobile Pass Issuance
1. Enter your mobile number for M-Pesa verification.
2. Complete the STK Push payment/accreditation prompt on your phone handset.
3. The platform instantly issues an encrypted **STRIDE™ Enterprise Digital Pass** containing:
   - Dynamic Scannable QR Code
   - M-Pesa Confirmation Reference & Serial ID
   - Member Voting Bracket Badge

---

### Step 3: Gate Check-in & Real-Time Quorum Tracking
1. At the AGM venue or online lobby, the QR code is scanned using any smartphone/tablet.
2. The system verifies membership authenticity in **<0.2 seconds**.
3. Real-time quorum progress updates immediately on the Executive Secretariat Boardroom Dashboard (verifying compliance with SASRA / SACCO Bylaws).

---

### Step 4: Encrypted Shareholder E-Voting & Real-Time Tally
1. Accredited members unlock their digital ballot paper.
2. Votes for Board Directors, Supervisory Committee, and Dividend Resolutions are cast securely.
3. Votes are cryptographically hashed and tallied in real-time, eliminating manual recounting and ballot tampering.
