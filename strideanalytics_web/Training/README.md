# Bank of Uganda (BoU) 5-Day Cyber Forensics Masterclass
### Repository & Practical Toolkit

Welcome to the **Bank of Uganda 5-Day Cyber Forensics Masterclass** repository. This directory contains course guides, technical slide references, hands-on lab environments, chain-of-custody documentation, and regulatory compliance material aligned with the Bank of Uganda’s sovereign security mandate.

---

## 📂 Repository Structure

- `DAY_1_FOUNDATIONS_AND_THREATS.md`: Principles of Digital Forensics, Central Bank Threat Models & Role of the Forensic Investigator.
- `DAY_2_NETWORK_FORENSICS_AND_IOCS.md`: Financial Cyber Attack Vectors, Indicators of Compromise (IoCs) & Packet Inspection.
- `DAY_3_EVIDENCE_ACQUISITION_AND_MEMORY.md`: Forensic Imaging, Volatile RAM Triage, NTFS/EXT4 Artifacts & Chain of Custody.
- `DAY_4_INCIDENT_RESPONSE_AND_MALWARE.md`: CSIRT Containment, Bank-Specific Playbooks & Financial Malware Analysis.
- `DAY_5_UGANDA_LAW_AND_EXECUTIVE_REPORTING.md`: Ugandan Cyber Jurisprudence (Computer Misuse Act), Court Admissibility & Boardroom Reporting.
- `labs/`: Interactive Python forensic tools, packet analyzers, hash verification suites, and lab exercises.
- `templates/`: Formal Chain of Custody forms and BoU Forensic Investigation Report templates.
